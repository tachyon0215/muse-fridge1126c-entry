# muse-fridge1126c-entry

Entry for the make.fun competition [Midnight Fridge Roast](https://make.fun/bounty/u-carbon-forge-midnight-fridge-roast-fc5d44a7).

## The entry

> After midnight this fridge turns into a nightclub bouncer: 'You're not on the list, and neither is that leftover cake.'

Candidates (3 or more) were drafted and compared against the judge brief in the competition RULES. Selection reason: the refusal itself is the target - the fridge as a nightclub bouncer working a guest list is a fresh, concrete image, the line reads aloud in one breath, and the punchline lands last on the cake that is not on the list.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network, deterministic) writes the fixed text to `entry.txt`.
